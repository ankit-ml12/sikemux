---
title: Sharing and the Stash
description: "Send links, text, pictures and files from any app on your phone to a chat, or keep them in your Stash for later."
---

Sikemux is in your phone's share menu. Share a link, some text, a picture, a video or a file from any app, then pick a chat for it, or keep it in your **Stash**.

## Sharing to a chat

The share sheet shows what you are sharing, a note you can add, your recent chats, and **New chat in** your projects. Pick one and what you shared lands in that chat's message box with your note, ready for you to send. It is never sent by itself. A new chat waits for you as well: its agent starts only once you begin writing.

The host the chat runs on puts it there as soon as it hears about it. A host that is asleep or offline does it the next time it connects to your account.

If the chat was closed in the meantime, or its project is no longer open on that host, what you shared stays in your Stash.

## The Stash

The Stash keeps what you shared for later. It belongs to your account, so every device and host signed in to it sees the same Stash.

On the Mac, **Stash** sits at the top of the left rail with how many items wait in it, and opens the Stash in a tab. Each item shows as a card: a link with its page's picture, title and site, a photo, the start of a text, or a file. **Add to chat** puts it in the chat you were last writing in, and its arrow, or **Add to chat** when no chat is open, picks another. **Open** opens a link in your browser or a file in its app, and copies a text; the bin removes the item. You can also drag a card onto any chat.

In a chat's message box, type `&` to list your Stash, and keep typing to narrow it by title, note, link or text; pick an item to add it. On your phone, the **Stash** button beside **+** in the message box opens your Stash, with what is new first. An item you add sits in the message box as a small preview with its picture and title; your note goes into what you are writing. A picture or file is copied to the host the chat runs on, and a link or text goes with the message when you send it.

On your phone, the **Stash** button counts what arrived since you last looked, on any device; **Add N new** at the top of the Stash adds them all. Opening the Stash counts as seeing them, everywhere.

You can stash a whole message too: hold the send button, or pick **Stash this prompt** in the Stash, and its text and the files you picked on the phone become one item, and the message box clears. **Undo** puts it back. Picking a stashed prompt later puts its words and files back in the message box.

An item you have used leaves the Stash and is deleted 30 days later. One item can be up to 25 MB, and your Stash can hold up to 1 GB.

## Who can see it

Your Stash is kept on Sikemux's account server, encrypted at rest with a key the server holds. It is not end-to-end encrypted: being signed in to your account is all the access it needs, and nobody else's account can reach it. Agents see an item only once you attach it to a message.
