# Sikemux v0.6.0-nightly.2

The second nightly of the new Sikemux app. Nightlies are signed and delivered exactly like stable releases, but they carry unreleased work and can break. Switch back to stable in Settings → About whenever you want; you keep the build you are on until a stable release passes it.

Your phone needs the nightly phone app, 0.2.0-nightly.3 or later, from TestFlight on iPhone or the Google Play testing track on Android, and nightly hosts link only with other nightly hosts.

## New since nightly.1

- **Chats keep up after sleeping.** An open chat that went to sleep shows what it says once it wakes, instead of stopping where it slept.
- **Tabs scroll.** When your tabs outgrow the tab row, it scrolls sideways and brings the chosen tab into view, rather than clipping the tabs past its edge.
- **Desk pages are the right size from the start.** An agent's first page reports the size the desk shows it at, and on Linux a tab no longer opens slightly too large on screens that aren't at 96 dpi.

For the complete patch history, compare [`v0.6.0-nightly.1...v0.6.0-nightly.2`](https://github.com/nodelike/sikemux/compare/v0.6.0-nightly.1...v0.6.0-nightly.2).
