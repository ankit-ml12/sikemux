# Sikemux v0.5.4-nightly.8

The eighth nightly on the 0.5.4 line. Nightlies are signed and delivered exactly like stable releases, but they carry unreleased work and can break. Switch back to stable in Settings → About whenever you want; you keep the build you are on until a stable release passes it.

## New since nightly.7

- **Sleeping chats wake again.** In nightly.7, a chat whose agent was asleep showed no history and refused messages with "ACP session is not running". Writing in it, sending, steering or editing a message now wakes it and delivers, and its history comes back as the agent starts.

For the complete patch history, compare [`v0.5.4-nightly.7...v0.5.4-nightly.8`](https://github.com/nodelike/sikemux/compare/v0.5.4-nightly.7...v0.5.4-nightly.8).
